68% leave without buying: the paradox of choice

Electronics retail is a paradox of choice problem. Staff are stretched during peak hours, and shoppers are left decoding spec sheets on their own.

Existing solutions dump information without helping process it. The opportunity: a conversational layer that translates raw specs into decisions. Contextual, instant, zero download friction.

02 · Research

Three weeks of in-store observation

⏱️

Avg. 23 mins of browsing

Most of that time spent re-reading the same spec cards, not exploring new options. Decision paralysis, not discovery.

🙋

Only 40% asked staff

Felt embarrassed, couldn't find staff, or didn't want pressure. A chatbot removes all three barriers simultaneously.

🔁

"Compare" was the #1 ask

"What's the difference between X and Y" was the most common staff question: a perfect conversational use case.

📱

QR > App store

Shoppers refused to download a retailer app. QR on the shelf, no install, no account, had near-zero friction in testing.

Persona research: Before writing a single response, I benchmarked the tone of other retail and conversational assistants to define Techie's own persona: a knowledgeable shelf-assistant, not a salesperson. Plain language, no upsell pressure, and quick to say "ask a human" when it didn't know.

06 · Impact

What this changes

Validated through 3 weeks of in-store observation and confirmed again in academic peer review: the barrier was never lack of information, it was lack of a way to process it.

68% of shoppers said information overload was why they'd leave without buying
40% only asked staff before Techie; the other 60% just left confused
0 app installs required, QR-triggered straight from the shelf

What I would measure if shipped: conversation-to-purchase conversion, "compare" intent frequency, and staff-handoff rate as a signal of where the bot's confidence should improve.

What I learned: the chatbot had to know when to step back. Conversational design is as much about silence as speech, and that same restraint became the core of Techie's persona.
