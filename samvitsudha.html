Samvit Sudha: NGO Website Redesign

A clean, purpose-driven redesign of the digital presence for Samvit Sudha, a non-profit organisation. The goal: make their mission instantly legible, reduce bounce rate, and improve the donation conversion path.

3x improvement in visual hierarchy from before to after
↓60% reduction in steps to reach the donation page
WCAG AA accessible colour contrast across all new components
1 designer (solo project): research through delivery

BEFORE

AFTER

Before: cluttered navigation, no clear mission, buried donate CTA. After: mission-first layout, impact numbers, clear donation path

01 · Problem

Good mission, invisible online

Samvit Sudha was doing meaningful work: education and livelihood programmes across rural Karnataka. But their website told almost none of that story. A first-time visitor couldn't answer two basic questions within 10 seconds: "What do they do?" and "How do I help?"

The site had too many navigation items, no clear hierarchy, and a donation flow buried under 4 clicks. For a non-profit, the website is the primary trust-building tool. This one was working against them.

02 · Design Principles

Three rules for non-profit design

🎯

Mission in 5 seconds

A visitor should understand exactly who Samvit Sudha is and what they do within the first scroll. If you need to read three paragraphs to understand the mission, it's not clear enough.

📊

Impact before ask

Show the work before requesting the donation. Numbers build trust: 1,200 students, 6 years, 14 villages. These earn the right to ask for money.

🔑

One CTA at a time

The old site had 8 different calls to action on the homepage. The redesign reduced that to one primary CTA per section, always in service of the same goal: donate or learn more.

03 · Key Changes

What changed and why

05 · Reflection

What designing for non-profits taught me

Constraints sharpen decisions. Non-profits rarely have design budgets for photography, illustration, or custom typefaces. This project taught me to make a system feel rich with just colour, space, and type, no decoration budget required.

Trust is a design material. Every layout decision, the placement of the impact numbers, the specific donation amount framing, the "tax-deductible" reassurance, was a trust decision. The UI design serves the organisation's credibility.
